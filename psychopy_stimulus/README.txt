Open repeated_viewing.psyexp in PsychoPy Builder and press Run.
Choose 2 or 3 in the repetitions field. Default: 2.
The selected MP4 contains all presentations in one continuous file, including audio.
PsychoPy shows a silent black screen for 30 seconds before the movie and
another silent black screen for 30 seconds after it. The cursor is hidden.
These periods occur once per run, not between repetitions.
Escape stops playback. Keep the MP4 files beside the experiment.
Data are saved in the data folder.

This is a basic playback experiment. Scanner triggers and
physiological synchronization have not been configured. Builder records the
whole movie presentation; internal repetition boundaries are encoded in the
video and are not separately measured display events.
Do not treat repeated presentations as independent participants.
